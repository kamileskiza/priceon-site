Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information PriceOn ("we", "us") collects when you visit priceon.tech, fill in one of our ad lead forms, or talk to our Telegram bot @priceonapp_bot, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Lead forms (Facebook / Instagram): your name and your answers to our short questions (for example, whether you use Telegram). We do not ask for your phone number or email in these forms.
  • Telegram: when you start our bot, Telegram shares your Telegram user ID, name, username and language with us, plus a short code that tells us which ad or page you came from.
  • Website: campaign tags in the link you clicked (such as utm_source), a random visit ID stored in your browser, and basic technical data (browser, device, pages viewed). If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your number and message.

How we use it

  • To open PriceOn for you in Telegram and help you get started.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, so we spend less on ones that don't.
  • To show our ads to people who already showed interest (for example, people who filled in a form), using the advertising platform's own tools.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only with services we use to run PriceOn: Meta (Facebook / Instagram ads and lead forms), Telegram, WhatsApp, our website host (Netlify) and analytics tools. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Lead form answers are kept for up to 12 months. Support conversations are kept as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.
  • You can stop the Telegram bot at any time (Stop and block bot in Telegram).
  • You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser.

To make a request, message us on Telegram at @priceonapp_bot and write "privacy request".

Age

PriceOn is for adults only (18+, or the legal age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
