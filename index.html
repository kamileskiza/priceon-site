FAQ

Quick answers

What is PriceOn?

A one-minute crypto price prediction game that runs as a Mini App inside Telegram.

How does a round work?

Each round has a start price. You choose your stake and tap PRICE UP or PRICE DOWN during the 15-second decision window. When the 45-second round ends, UP wins if the price closed above the start, DOWN wins if it closed below.

How long is a round?

45 seconds, after a 15-second decision window — about one minute per cycle.

Which markets can I predict?

Currently SOL/USDC. Available markets are always shown in the app.

How do I start?

Tap “Play on Telegram”. PriceOn opens inside Telegram — choose Demo and make your first prediction.

Do I need Telegram?

Yes. PriceOn runs only as a Telegram Mini App — no separate download or account.

What is Demo?

Practice mode with virtual Demo SOL. It can't be deposited, withdrawn or converted to real SOL, and you can reset it to 10.00 at any time.

What is Live?

Live mode uses the SOL balance in your in-app wallet on Solana Mainnet. Trades are final after each round.

How does deposit work?

Switch to Live and use the Deposit button to add SOL to your in-app wallet.

Are there fees?

Yes — a flat 1% of your stake per prediction. It's shown before you confirm.

How does referral work?

Share your personal code from the Profile tab. Invites, your monthly estimate and total paid are tracked there. Rewards are paid on a monthly cycle under the terms shown in the app.

Is this financial advice?

No. PriceOn is a prediction game. Nothing here is investment or financial advice.

What are the risks?

In Live mode you stake real SOL and can lose it. Short-term price moves are unpredictable. Start in Demo and only stake what you can afford to lose.
